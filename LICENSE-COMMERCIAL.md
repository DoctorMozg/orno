## Who needs a commercial license

The AGPL-3.0 is a strong copyleft license. Among other obligations, it requires
any party that conveys a modified version of orno, or offers orno's
functionality to third parties over a network, to make the corresponding
source code of that version available to those users under the AGPL-3.0.

You likely need a commercial license if you want to:

- **Embed or redistribute orno** inside a proprietary product, appliance,

  without making the surrounding product available under the AGPL-3.0.
- **Offer orno as a hosted or managed service** to third parties without
  publishing the corresponding source code under the AGPL-3.0.
- **Modify orno** and distribute those modifications to customers without
  releasing the modifications under the AGPL-3.0.

You do **not** need a commercial license if you use orno unmodified inside
your own organization (including on self-hosted CI), or if you are prepared

A commercial license replaces the AGPL-3.0 obligations for the paying
